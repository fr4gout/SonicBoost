One-time audio setup

To boost all Windows sound, SonicBoost listens to a free virtual cable and plays the boosted sound on your speakers.

  1. Install VB-Audio Virtual Cable (free), then restart your PC.
  2. In Windows Sound settings set CABLE Input as the default output.
  3. In SonicBoost Settings, pick your real speakers as Play to.
  4. Turn your speakers' own volume to 100%. SonicBoost's boost is added on top of it.
